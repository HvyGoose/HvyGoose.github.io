Privacy Policy — No Frills Media Organizer

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Publisher: Ganso Gordo Productions

The short version

No Frills Media Organizer runs entirely on your computer. Your files, your file names, your photos, your videos, and everything the app learns about them never leave your device. We cannot see them because they are never sent to us.

What the app does

No Frills Media Organizer scans folders you choose, helps you find duplicate photos and videos, and organizes your media into a library you select. All analysis — hashing, thumbnails, similarity comparison — happens locally on your PC. The app stores its working data in a local database on your machine.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No account is required and no sign-in exists.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No file contents, file names, thumbnails, hashes, or folder paths are transmitted off your device.
  • No advertising identifiers, cookies, or third-party trackers.
  • The app makes no network connections except: (a) checking for application updates if you enable that option, and (b) any action you explicitly initiate that requires the internet.

Your files

  • The app moves, deletes, or skips files only after you explicitly approve those actions.
  • Deleted files are permanently removed; the app clearly marks these actions before you confirm.
  • The app keeps a local operation log so the "undo" feature can reverse organize/move actions. This log stays on your device and is never transmitted.

Purchases

If you purchase No Frills Media Organizer through the Microsoft Store, the transaction is processed entirely by Microsoft under the Microsoft Store's own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Cloud-synced folders

If you choose to scan folders managed by cloud services (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, etc.), those services' own software may upload or download files according to their settings. That behavior is governed by your agreements with those providers, not by us. The app never talks to any cloud service directly.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the effective date will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
